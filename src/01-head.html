What this is

The file tools other sites meter, watermark or lock behind a subscription — free, unlimited, and running entirely inside your browser.

How it works

Your files are opened and rewritten by JavaScript on this page. Nothing is uploaded, so there is no server to pay for and no reason to charge you or count your tasks.

Honest limits

Big files are limited by your own memory, not by us. Heavier tools — OCR, background removal, video, archives — download a one-off engine the first time you use them, then run offline. Anything we can't do well, we say so on the tool.
